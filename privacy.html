Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026

Unified-MCP-MailApi ("the app") connects an AI assistant you choose to the mailboxes and calendars you link. This policy explains what data the app accesses, what it does with it, and what it never does.

Data the app accesses

  • Google user data: with your permission, Gmail messages, threads and attachments (gmail.readonly), and calendar events (calendar.readonly). If you link an account with full access, it can also create drafts, send mail you confirm, change message labels such as read/unread or archived, and create calendar events.
  • Microsoft user data: the equivalent mail and calendar data through Microsoft Graph, plus your basic profile (name and email address).
  • Account identifiers: the email address of each linked account, and the access you granted.

How the data is used

  • Mail and calendar data is fetched only when you, through your AI assistant, ask for it, and is passed only to that assistant to answer your request.
  • The app does not store message contents. It stores only what it needs to stay connected: the OAuth refresh token for each linked account, kept in a file only you can read on your own computer, or encrypted at rest when you run it on your own server.
  • The app's activity log records which action ran and when, never message contents, subjects or addresses.

What the app never does

  • It does not sell, rent or share your data with anyone.
  • It does not use your data for advertising, and it does not use it to train AI models.
  • No person reads your mail. The app is operated by its user, not by a third party.
  • It does not send email or create events without your explicit confirmation.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Unified-MCP-MailApi's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your AI assistant

Data you ask about is shown to the AI assistant you connected (for example Claude or ChatGPT). That assistant's own privacy policy governs how it handles the conversation.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Use the app's unlink_account action to remove one account and its stored token, or delete_my_data to remove everything.
  • You can also revoke access at any time in your Google account or Microsoft account; the app then loses access immediately.

Security

All connections to Google and Microsoft use HTTPS. Refresh tokens are encrypted at rest on servers, and access is limited to the permissions you granted when linking each account.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Questions: contact Daniel Jonathan on GitHub or use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen.
