Unified-MCP-MailApi

An MCP server for your mailboxes

Unified-MCP-MailApi lets an AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT and other clients that speak the Model Context Protocol) search and read several mailboxes at once: Gmail, Outlook.com and Microsoft 365. You ask one question and get one answer across all your accounts.

What it does

  • Searches and reads mail and calendar events across all linked accounts, newest first.
  • Read-only by default. Drafting and sending are only available for accounts you link with full access.
  • Never sends mail on its own: every send needs your confirmation outside the AI's control.
  • Runs on your own computer or your own server. There is no shared cloud service holding your mail.

How you connect an account

You sign in with Google or Microsoft and grant access. You can remove access at any time, from the tool or from your Google account or Microsoft account settings.
